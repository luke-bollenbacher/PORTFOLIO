Background

I was born in Denver, Colorado and grew up in Boulder County until I was thirteen — the kind of upbringing that puts the Rockies in your peripheral vision and trail dust on your boots.

At thirteen, my family moved to Klein, Texas, where I attended Klein Oak High School for 9th grade, finishing Summa Cum Laude, before we moved again — this time to Summit, New Jersey, where I finished high school at Summit High School, graduating Magna Cum Laude.

Through all of it, Colorado has remained home in the way that matters. Non-school time has always been spent back there with my father, in the mountains where I learned to pay attention to the physical world.

Three states, two coasts, one trajectory — from Boulder to Aggieland.

Now I'm at Texas A&M — one of the largest engineering programs in the country, founded in 1876, with a culture that takes engineering seriously. It's the right place to build what I want to build.
